in development · personal project

FinManAI

An AI-powered personal finance advisor built for India. Users share their full financial picture and get personalised guidance on saving, investing, debt and insurance, including instruments like PPF, NPS and EPF.

Advice comes from deterministic rule engines, so every recommendation is traceable. A compliance gate checks it, and only then does an AI layer explain it in plain language. Onboarding uses Google sign-in and PAN-based KYC.
